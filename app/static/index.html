BFSI Hackathon Solution Alignment

How ClaimGuard Arena maps directly to the Google Cloud AI Builder Cup BFSI Problem Statement:

1. Multi-Agent Reasoning

Uses Gemini on Vertex AI for multimodal consistency analysis between police narrative, photo descriptions, and itemized repair estimates.

2. Zero False Positives (Decoy)

Explicitly models legitimate high-volume repair shops (CityFix Motors) to ensure busy businesses are cleared rather than wrongly penalized.

3. Adversarial Red-Teaming

Solves the LLM evaluation problem by pitting an automated AI Attacker against the Defender in a continuous benchmarking Arena.
